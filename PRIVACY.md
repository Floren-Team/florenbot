# Privacy Policy for Florenbot

At Florenbot, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed by the bot. This policy outlines how data is handled.

## Data Collection
- **Runtime Data:** Florenbot utilizes MySQL for persistent state management and Redis for caching. This may include user identifiers, chat states, or interaction logs necessary for the core functionality of the bot and game engine.
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to outside parties.

## Data Security
We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your data when running, deploying, or interacting with the bot.

## Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the GitHub repository.
